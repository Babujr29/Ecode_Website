Welcome to ECODE

The Economics and Data Science Club

Welcome to ECODE, the premier student body dedicated to bridging the gap between economic theory and modern data analytics. We are a community of critical thinkers and innovators. Our club fosters an environment where students can explore the intricacies of data visualization, econometrics, and financial modeling. Through workshops, hackathons, and research projects, we empower our members to turn raw data into actionable economic insights. Join us in redefining the future of Economics.
