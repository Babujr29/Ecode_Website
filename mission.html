Mission & Vision

Our Vision

To build a vibrant academic and professional community that empowers students to use economics and data science as tools for informed decision-making, innovation, and social impact.

Our Mission

• To promote interdisciplinary learning by integrating economics with data science and analytical thinking.

• To provide hands-on learning experiences through events like HACK that encourage problem-solving and collaboration.

• To expose students to industry insights and career pathways through expert-led talks and workshops.

• To cultivate a culture of research, curiosity, and critical inquiry within the student community.

• To bridge the gap between theory and real-world application in economics and data-driven domains.
